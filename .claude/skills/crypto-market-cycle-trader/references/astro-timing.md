# Astrological timing overlay — expanded notes and caveats

**Read this first**: there is no demonstrated causal mechanism linking planetary positions or lunar phases to asset prices. Nothing below should be used as a standalone entry/exit trigger. It is documented because a segment of traders in this space — including strands of Gann-derived technical analysis and parts of crypto-Twitter — use it as a low-confidence, sentiment/volatility timing overlay, and this skill's job is to represent that honestly rather than either oversell or ignore it.

## Mercury retrograde

- An optical/orbital-mechanics effect where Mercury appears to move backward against the background stars for a few weeks at a time, several times a year.
- Some crypto astrologers claim a pattern: during bear markets, Bitcoin has seen sharp drawdowns coinciding with Mercury retrograde windows (two cited examples: a ~30% fall around the March 2018 retrograde and a ~28% fall around the July 2018 retrograde); during bull markets, the same windows are claimed to have coincided with strong bullish impulses instead.
- Read charitably, the claim is about **volatility clustering and trend-continuation/exhaustion around these windows**, not a fixed direction — which is exactly why this skill treats it only as a "expect chop, tighten stops, reduce size" flag, never a directional call.
- Mainstream/skeptical view: price action during these windows is far better explained by macro drivers (Fed policy, ETF flows, leverage unwinds) that happen to coincide, and any correlation is likely selection bias from looking back over many retrograde windows and only remembering the dramatic ones.

## Full moon / new moon

- Some traders watch full-moon dates as candidate local-top windows and new-moon dates as candidate local-bottom windows, on the theory that lunar cycles influence crowd psychology/emotional extremes.
- Used here only as a cross-check: if a full moon lines up with an already-extended RSI reading and a Wyckoff distribution structure (lens 2/3 already flashing "possible top"), it can modestly raise conviction on a reversal thesis that's already there — it should never be the reason a thesis exists.

## Gann time-price theory

- W.D. Gann's framework treats time and price as interchangeable/vibrating together — anniversary dates of major highs/lows, and specific time-cycle counts, are watched as candidate turning-point windows, alongside price-geometry techniques (angles, squares) for target levels.
- In crypto-specific applications, this has been extended (e.g., by "Gann Astro Trader"-style analysts) to combine planetary longitude conversions with these time cycles for BTC and other majors.
- Same rule as above: a Gann-anniversary date is a "pay closer attention to structure here" flag, not a trade signal in isolation.

## The hard rule this skill enforces

Astrology-derived signals may only ever modulate **risk parameters** (position size, stop tightness, willingness to hold through chop) around a trade thesis already built from price action, technical analysis, and cycle context. They must never be the stated reason to open or close a position on their own.
