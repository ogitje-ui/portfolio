# BTC 4-year halving cycle history

## The mechanism

Bitcoin's block subsidy halves roughly every 4 years (every 210,000 blocks). This is a hard-coded supply shock, not a market prediction — the "cycle" is the market's historical behavioral response to it.

## Halvings

| Halving | Date | Block subsidy after |
|---|---|---|
| 1st | Nov 2012 | 50 → 25 BTC |
| 2nd | Jul 2016 | 25 → 12.5 BTC |
| 3rd | May 2020 | 12.5 → 6.25 BTC |
| 4th | Apr 2024 | 6.25 → 3.125 BTC |

## The historical pattern (pre-2024)

Across the first three halving cycles:
- Bull-market tops clustered **12–18 months after** the halving.
- Peak-to-trough bear drawdowns ran **77–85%** every cycle.
- Each bear bottomed **before the next halving** arrived, resetting the loop.
- Bear-bottom to bear-bottom, and bull-top to bull-top, both spanned roughly 4 years.

## 2024–2026 cycle: where the pattern broke

- Bitcoin's cycle-high after the April 2024 halving printed at roughly $126,296 on October 6, 2025 — inside the historical 12–18 month top window.
- What did **not** match prior cycles: the year following the halving (2025) closed lower than it opened for the first time in Bitcoin's history — roughly -6% — rather than the "always green" post-halving year seen in 2013, 2017, and 2021.
- From the October 2025 high, price declined about 46.7% to roughly $67,550 by mid-February 2026 — a drawdown inside historical range but arriving faster/earlier relative to the halving than prior cycles.
- A naive peak-to-trough-interval model (using the 2021 top → 2022 bottom gap of 365 days, applied to the October 2025 top) points to a cycle-low window around **October 2026** — offered here as one illustrative model, not a forecast.

## Why the cycle is contested for this era

- Spot BTC ETF flows introduced a large, relatively cycle-agnostic institutional buyer base that didn't exist in prior cycles.
- Growing corporate/treasury accumulation (MicroStrategy-style balance-sheet holders) changes the supply/demand dynamics versus a market dominated by retail and miners.
- The mainstream view among analysts is split: some argue the 4-year cycle is "arriving close to on time" and still the dominant rhythm; others argue its *amplitude* is moderating even if the rough timing rhyme persists.

## How this skill uses cycle context

Treat "months since halving" and the historical top/bottom clustering as a **prior that adjusts confidence**, not a mechanical trade trigger. A late-cycle euphoric market with lens 2/3 signals also stretched is a much higher-conviction "reduce risk" read than the cycle timing alone would justify; conversely, a cycle-implied bottom window with no price-action confirmation is not a buy signal by itself.
