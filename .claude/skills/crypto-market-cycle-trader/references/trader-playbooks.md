# Trader playbooks

Expanded methodology notes on the traders the main skill draws from. These are public, self-described trading approaches synthesized from their content — not verbatim quotes, not copy-trading instructions, and not a claim that following them guarantees results.

## Trader Mayne

- Trading crypto since 2013; core discipline is **price action** — support/resistance, range trading, and liquidity sweeps rather than indicator-heavy systems.
- Went through a full boom-bust-rebuild arc: quit a job to trade full time, blew up and had to go back to work, rebuilt a real track record, then lost a large amount in the FTX collapse and recovered again. The consistent thread in his own retrospectives is psychology and risk control mattering more than any single setup.
- Co-founded **Breakout**, a crypto-native prop trading firm (acquired by Kraken in 2025) — funded-trader model built around passing a risk-managed evaluation, which reinforces the risk-first framing above.
- Content mixes pure PA reads with market-cycle/sentiment commentary, and occasionally references Gann-style time/price and astrological timing as a minor overlay on top of a PA thesis — never as the thesis itself. That's the model this skill's astro lens follows.
- **Skill takeaway**: liquidity-sweep-aware support/resistance trading (lens 2), strict risk control regardless of conviction, astrology treated as a size/stop modifier at most.

Sources: [tradermayne.com](https://www.tradermayne.com/home), [Trader Mayne — YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/tradermayne), [Mayne biography — Traders Union](https://tradersunion.com/persons/mayne/)

## TraderXO

- Publishes under the handle XO / @Trader_XO; content centers on **Wyckoff method** market structure — accumulation, markup, distribution, markdown — applied to crypto order flow.
- Emphasis on "smart money" liquidity concepts: composite-operator-style accumulation/distribution ranges, springs and upthrusts, and reading volume alongside price to judge whether a breakout is real or a liquidity grab.
- Also writes about order flow, strategy development, and trading psychology/mindset on Substack, framing structure-reading as a skill built over many repetitions rather than a fixed indicator set.
- **Skill takeaway**: this is the direct source for lens 2 (Wyckoff phase identification, spring/upthrust liquidity sweeps, structure-break confirmation via volume).

Sources: [XO on X](https://twitter.com/Trader_XO), [TraderXO Substack](https://www.traderxo.com/welcome), [Wyckoff method overview — Wyckoff Analytics](https://www.wyckoffanalytics.com/demand/trading-the-crypto-market-with-the-wyckoff-method/), [Wyckoff method — dYdX](https://www.dydx.xyz/crypto-learning/wyckoff-method)

## Other reference points used for context

- **Arthur Hayes** (BitMEX co-founder) — widely followed for macro liquidity, leverage-cycle, and central-bank-driven risk-appetite commentary; useful as the "why now" macro layer around the mechanical 4-year cycle.
- **Michael Saylor** — the highest-profile cycle-agnostic accumulation thesis (buy and hold through cycles rather than trade them); useful as a deliberate counterweight so this skill doesn't over-fit to cycle-timing as the only valid approach.
- **On-chain/derivatives analytics tradition** (Glassnode-, CryptoQuant-, Coinglass-style tooling) — not one trader but the data layer that lens 4 (funding, open interest, liquidations) is built from.

## How this skill uses them

None of the above is prescriptive "do exactly what X does." The skill extracts the *evidence-weighted* parts of each approach — PA/structure reading, disciplined risk sizing, macro cycle awareness, derivatives-based timing — and explicitly downgrades the parts (astrology, pure sentiment) that lack a demonstrated causal mechanism to a risk-modifier role only.
